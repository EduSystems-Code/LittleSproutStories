📊 The Research

Statistics & Data

Why early reading and social-emotional skills matter — from real public research — and an honest account of what LittleSprout does and doesn't track about your child.

Why early literacy matters

These come from ongoing, publicly published research programs. Rather than restate numbers that change with every new report, each card links straight to the primary source so you can see the current figures yourself.

📈 NAEP — The Nation's Report Card

The federal government's ongoing assessment of what U.S. students know in reading, by grade and by state, tracked over time.

nationsreportcard.gov →

👶 Annie E. Casey Foundation — KIDS COUNT

State-by-state child well-being data, including 3rd-grade reading proficiency, updated annually and free to browse.

aecf.org/kids-count →

🏫 Maryland State Department of Education

Maryland's own school and student performance data, including reading outcomes by school and district.

reportcard.msde.maryland.gov →

💛 CASEL — Social-Emotional Learning

The leading research clearinghouse on social-emotional learning outcomes in early childhood and K-12 settings.

casel.org →

We link instead of quoting a number here on purpose: a specific percentage printed on this page would go stale the moment the next report comes out — and a number should never stand in for a claim about a real child.

LittleSprout's own numbers

What we can honestly say about the project itself, right now.

📚 The library

14 interactive storybooks, 8 story pages each (plus a cover and an end-of-book badge screen), and 3 games, built on a shared “Double Engine” reading + feelings model.

🎭 The cast

4 main characters across 5 expressions and 12 poses each, plus 11 named community helpers the series is built around.

👀 Readers & usage Not yet available

LittleSprout collects no reader data (see our privacy policy), and as of this writing the site hasn't yet been used outside internal development. Real usage stats will appear here once that changes — honestly, not projected.

We will never post a statistic here we can't point to a real source for — house rule, not just a slogan.
