Privacy

We don’t collect anything.

The short version, in plain language — then the details.

The short version

  • No accounts. No sign-up, no login, no email address.
  • No personal information is collected from anyone — child or adult — unless a parent chooses to request a physical sticker reward (see below); that is the one exception, and it only happens if you ask for it.
  • No advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No analytics or tracking pixels.
  • Reading progress stays on your own device and never reaches us.

Who this is for

LittleSprout is made for children roughly ages 4–7 and the grown-ups reading alongside them. Because our audience includes children under 13, we designed the site so that the question of children’s data mostly doesn’t arise: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to fill in.

What we store on your device

When your child finishes a book, the site saves a small note in your own browser’s local storage so the badge shows up next time. That record contains only:

  • which books have been finished, and the badge earned
  • the date each one was finished
  • whether the easier-to-read text setting is switched on

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to any server. Clearing your browser data, or using the “Clear saved progress” button on the badge shelf, erases it completely.

Note: because this is stored per-device and per-browser, progress will not follow your child from a tablet to a laptop. That is a deliberate trade-off — syncing would require accounts, and accounts would require collecting personal information.

Sticker rewards (the one exception)

A family that finishes all 14 books can choose to request a physical sticker pack of the four friends, mailed to a real address. This is entirely opt-in — nothing about it happens unless a parent actively starts the request themselves.

Requesting one asks for a name and mailing address, and requires verifying you’re an adult through Stripe Identity (a government-ID check, handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store the ID itself, only Stripe’s confirmation that verification succeeded). That address is used only to mail the stickers, and is permanently deleted 90 days after the package ships. It is never used for anything else, never sold, and never linked back to your child’s reading progress (which, as above, never leaves your device in the first place).

Why identity verification at all, for a free reward? Because this is a children’s site, collecting even a parent’s mailing address puts us in COPPA’s “verifiable parental consent” territory. We chose the most rigorous method available to a small independent site rather than a lighter one, on purpose.

Read-aloud narration

The “Read to me” button uses speech capability already built into your browser and device. No microphone is used, no recording takes place, and nothing your child says or does is captured.

Depending on the browser and the voice selected, the text of the page may be processed by your operating system or, for some cloud-based voices, by your browser vendor. That is the same mechanism any website using built-in speech relies on, and it is governed by your device and browser settings rather than by us. If you prefer to avoid it entirely, simply don’t use the button.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard server request information such as IP addresses in order to deliver the page; see GitHub’s own privacy documentation for details. We do not receive, store, or have access to that information.

We load fonts from Google Fonts. We include no advertising networks, no social media trackers, and no analytics services.

Some pages link out to Maryland early-childhood organizations as a convenience. Those are independent websites with their own privacy practices, and we are not affiliated with them.

Your rights as a parent

Under COPPA, parents have the right to review, delete, and refuse further collection of a child’s personal information. Reading progress saved on your device is fully under your control and can be cleared at any time from the badge shelf or through your browser settings. If you’ve requested a sticker reward, that mailing address is deleted automatically 90 days after shipping (see above) — to have it removed sooner, or to ask what we hold, reach out through the contact method below.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if accounts or classroom features are added — this page will be updated before the change goes live, and the summary at the top will say plainly what is different.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else about the site, can be raised through the project’s GitHub repository.
