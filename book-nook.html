📚 Pass it on

The Book Nook

When your reader outgrows their early books, those pages still have a lot of first days of school left in them. Here is how to send them on to the next new reader.

Where to pass books on, close to home

The lowest-effort, lowest-cost options are almost always local — no shipping, and the books land with a child nearby.

  • A Little Free Library. The little book boxes on neighbourhood posts. Find one at littlefreelibrary.org/map and leave a few titles.
  • A Title I classroom or your local elementary school. Early-grade teachers almost always want more decodable readers for a classroom library. Ask the front office or a teacher you know.
  • A paediatrician's or clinic waiting room. Many keep a book basket; some partner with Reach Out and Read.
  • A family shelter, food pantry, or diaper bank. Ask whether they can put children's books in with what families take home.
  • A little one you know. A younger cousin, a neighbour, a friend's new baby — a hand-me-down book with a note inside is a small, real gift.

Trusted programs that take books by mail

If you would rather ship a box, these are established nonprofits that redistribute gently used children's books. Send books via USPS Media Mail — it is much cheaper for books than regular parcel post.

📗 The Children's Book Bank

Collects gently used children's books and gets them to lower-income families and classrooms.

childrensbookbank.org →

🍪 Milk + Bookies

A national nonprofit that gets new and gently used books to children through youth-serving charities.

milkandbookies.org →

🌍 Books for Africa

Ships donated books to schools and libraries across the African continent; accepts gently used current titles.

booksforafrica.org →

🏫 Scholastic Book Donations

Scholastic's directory of vetted book-donation and literacy organisations, sortable by need.

scholastic.com/bookdonations →

📖 ALA donation directory

The American Library Association's maintained list of national and regional groups that accept book donations.

libguides.ala.org/book-donations →

📚 First Book

Provides free and low-cost books to programs serving kids in need; also a place classrooms can register to receive.

firstbook.org →
