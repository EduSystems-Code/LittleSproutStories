How it works

Two engines in every story

LittleSprout pairs learning to read with learning to feel — so each adventure grows the whole child.

📖

Decodable reading

Short, sound-it-out sentences and repeated sight words build confidence on every page.

⭐

Tap-to-learn words

Bolded vocabulary words open kid-friendly definitions, turning new words into known words.

💕

Big-feelings practice

Characters model naming emotions and making kind choices — social-emotional learning woven into the story.

🎯

Playful checks

Hotspots, sorting, and questions keep readers active and reward them with badges, not pressure.

Made for the people who read along

Safe, ad-free, and screen-time you can feel good about. Snuggle up and tap through a story together, or let an independent reader explore.

Aligned to early-literacy standards for phonics and sight words, with SEL themes that fit morning meeting and centers.

🧒 Kids

Bright friends, real choices, and badges to earn. Big buttons and read-along text make it easy to play solo.
