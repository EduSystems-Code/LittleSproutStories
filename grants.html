Grants & funding for families

LittleSprout is free to read online. These are real outside programs that help cover the wider cost of early literacy — books, child care, and school-readiness support — for Maryland families and beyond.

Dolly Parton's Imagination Library

Mails one free, age-appropriate book a month to every enrolled child from birth to age five, in participating areas.

imaginationlibrary.com →

Judy Centers (Maryland)

Maryland's school-readiness centers, based at Title I schools, offering free early learning and family support services.

MSDE Judy Centers →

Maryland Family Network / LOCATE

Help finding and affording child care, including how to apply for the Child Care Scholarship Program.

marylandfamilynetwork.org →

First Book

A national nonprofit that provides free and low-cost books to programs serving children from families facing financial need.

firstbook.org →

Grants & funding for classrooms and programs

If you're a teacher, librarian, or early-childhood program looking to bring decodable, SEL-themed reading into your room, these are established places to start looking for funding.

DonorsChoose

Teachers post classroom funding requests — including books and reading technology — directly to public donors.

donorschoose.org →

IMLS Grants to States

The Institute of Museum and Library Sciences funds state library agencies, which in turn fund local literacy programming.

imls.gov →

Barbara Bush Foundation

Funds family literacy programs nationally, with a focus on programs serving families with the greatest need.

barbarabush.org →
